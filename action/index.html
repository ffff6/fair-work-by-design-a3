Action Plan

Protect people. Test the System; Correct the Process

It should happen fast enough to avoid any damage and be robust enough to withstand analysis.

05/05
1

Protect the evidence

Compile case studies of similar cases, rating disparities and prior reports. Anonymize employees' identities and restrict their access.

2

Suspend high-impact applications

Require that decisions on performance reviews, pay, and promotions be put on hold until human evaluation is made.

3

Take appropriate action

Write a report on the risks involved and send it to the Human Resources, the compliance department, or the ethics hotline. If the executives are not willing to cooperate, you have the right to escalate.

4

Perform audits and correction

Audit the tool to check for biases and lack of validity. Take corrective measures, inform the employees about this situation, and provide them with the chance to appeal.

5

Constant governance

Appoint a person who will handle the matter, make the explanations clear, perform audits of the group, and discontinue using the tool if the results are beyond repair.

My decision

I will not allow the ratings to go unchecked.

I will point out the documented disparity, ask for human intervention immediately, and then take it through the appropriate channels. It may cause some process problems, but it gives the company the opportunity to solve the issue.

The strategy is based on the govern-map-measure-manage cycle from NIST and OECD Fairness Guidelines. (NIST, 2023; OECD, 2024).
