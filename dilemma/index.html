The Ethical Dilemma

A pattern that the executive refuses to recognize

Silence preserves the status quo; unsupported allegations will not be enough. Ethical behavior transforms a hunch into something that can be assessed.

Scenario

The ratings seem unbiased.
The results certainly are not.

A company installs an artificial intelligence system for performance evaluations. Workers who belong to underrepresented groups receive inferior ratings despite putting in the same amount of effort as their coworkers and are put under review.

Fairness

Equal contributions should be treated equally. Historical bias cannot be a valid metric.

Transparency

It is essential that employees know AI is being used and what considerations are at stake.

Accountability

The organization, and not the software vendor or algorithm, is responsible for employment decisions.

Privacy

Collection and analysis of employee data must only include relevant, legal and proportionate data.

The Singapore Model AI Governance Framework says AI should be explained, transparent and fair, and have an appropriate level of human intervention relative to any harm caused (IMDA & PDPC, 2020).
