Assessing the technology

AI may increase reliability.
It can also scale up error.

Corporations employ AI technologies in order to make predictions regarding efficiency through productivity analysis, results of projects, attendance, and surveys. Used appropriately, this method will enable less bureaucracy, trend analysis, and faster response.

However, in accordance with the International Labor Organization, HR systems will rely on uncertain goals, biased data, and undisclosed algorithms. The algorithm trained by previous decisions will perpetuate inequality because, for instance, the ‘visibility’ criterion will foster improved relations with managers rather than increased efficiency (Berg & Johnston, 2025).

Evaluation: AI should be used to inform a manager, not replace managerial judgement. Its value lies in valid measurement, representative data, and continued evaluation.
